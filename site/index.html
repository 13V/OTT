OT+T

OT+T is a phone carrier that runs on a memecoin: a creator tax on every trade funds a treasury, and the treasury spends it on mobile data for the people who traded, not on a payout for the people who only hold. A memecoin can go to zero, and most do. Nothing here is financial advice.
