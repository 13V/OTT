The repo

OT+T links a memecoin to mobile data. Once active, creator tax collected in one week funds a variable data budget for eligible holders the next week. Unused credit expires and cannot be withdrawn as cash. See Status for current availability. A memecoin can go to zero; nothing here is financial advice.
