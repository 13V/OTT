The repo

OT+T is a phone carrier that runs on a memecoin: a creator tax on every trade funds a treasury, and every week last week's tax becomes a data budget split among holders by their share of the circulating supply — not a payout for trading, and not a dividend for holding either, since it expires at the end of the week it was published for. A memecoin can go to zero, and most do. Nothing here is financial advice.
