# Holder app artwork

These assets were created with the built-in ImageGen tool for the October 2026 app redesign.
The original generated PNGs remain in the task's Codex generated-images directory. Production
WebP files were encoded with Pillow; the logo retains its transparent alpha channel.

The shared art direction follows the approved three-screen mockup and the existing OTT holder:
chunky dark curly hair, a navy jacket and backpack, cream trousers and white shoes. Home and Kit
include the same cream and orange dog. Materials are handmade matte clay, with imperfect edges,
fingerprints and warm soft lighting. The palette combines orange, cream, navy and muted greens.
Scene compositions use a central subject in a landscape world, with no baked-in UI or balances.

The generation briefs were:

- **Home:** the holder walking his dog with his phone in a sunny miniature clay park. Include a
  path, leafy trees, flowers and a small bench. Keep the character safely framed in a 16:9 scene.
- **Japan:** the same holder and backpack on a handmade Japanese street, using his phone.
  Include small storefronts, lanterns, plants, tactile paving and distant pedestrians. No phone booth.
- **Connection kit:** the holder and dog at a warm wooden table, checking a phone beside a navy
  backpack, map and cream cup. Keep the scene compact, immersive and legible at mobile size.
- **Logo:** a small handmade orange rounded clay plaque with raised warm cream letters spelling
  exactly **OT+T**, viewed almost straight on. Use gentle surface imperfections, subtle shadows,
  a tight landscape composition and a genuinely transparent background.

Credit amounts, country labels, package sizes, buttons and setup instructions are rendered as
HTML. The artwork never encodes wallet balances, activation credentials or installation status.
